## 👋 About me

I build and deploy **computer vision systems** for action recognition, object detection and multi-object tracking, with a focus on **resource-constrained edge hardware**. My work sits where a model stops being a notebook result and starts being a system.

- 🎓 **M.Sc. Artificial Intelligence Engineering** at Universität Passau, Germany
- 📄 **3 IEEE conference papers** on action recognition and real-time human tracking (2023 to 2025)
- 🧱 **Co-building [i-Brick](https://ckteja.github.io/i-brick/)**, in-wall building-moisture sensing, pilot opening Q4 2026
- 💼 Previously **Deep Learning Engineer Intern** at Resolute AI, Bangalore
- 🔭 **Open to working-student and Master's thesis roles** in applied ML and computer vision
- 📍 Passau, Germany · English · German (A2, working toward B1)
